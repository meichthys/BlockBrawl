    Surprise catalogue

    Gold ? blocks ride in on some pieces (and on the odd garbage line). Clear a line holding two of them and one of these lands on your opponent. Every surprise tells you how to shake it.

    Controls

    BLOCK BRAWL

    Play someone else online from anywhere, or share one keyboard or touchscreen. Clear lines to bury your opponent. Clear two gold ? blocks in one line to hand them a surprise.

    Connecting…

    Each player gets a button pad under their board. On a phone lying flat between you, tap Face each other so Player 1's half turns toward them.
